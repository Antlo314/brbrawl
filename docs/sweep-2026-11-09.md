# Full sweep, 2026-11-09

What changed in the sweep of story mode, the menus and the tests. The backup of the code as it was before is in
`MM Game/originals/code-backup-2026-11-09/`.

## 1. Story mode is now a platform brawler

Story mode (`brawl.js`, levels in `campaign.js`) had flat streets: walk right, clear the wave, walk right. It now has a world to move through.

| New thing | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms | Crates, stages, trucks, scaffolds and docks you jump onto. Melee enemies jump up after you (up to 130 high). Ranged enemies stay down. | `floorAt`, `hop`, `worldStep` in `brawl.js` |
| Pits | Gaps in a roof, open manholes. Fall in and you lose health and come back at the edge. Enemies knocked in are ringed out. | `pitAt`, `fallIn`, `ringOut` |
| Pushable crates | Walk into one to push it. Stand on it to reach higher. Push it onto a floor plate to hold the plate down. | `pushCrate`, `shove` |
| Puzzles | One per level, in a calm zone: its first wave only, no more waves until the puzzle is solved (see the table below). | `TASKS.plates / sequence / lights / switch / carry` |
| Mixtapes | One hidden tape per level, usually up on a platform. Find all twelve for the CRATE DIGGER badge. | `save.tapes`, badge `story_tapes` |
| HUD column | Under the score: zone pips, the task strip, a hint strip and a one-line speaker strip. | `brawl.js` HUD |

The world is switched off in the replay modes (Endless, Boss Rush, Daily Challenge), which stay pure fighting.

### The five puzzles

| Puzzle | How you solve it | Solo | Two players |
|---|---|---|---|
| `plates` | Hold every floor plate down at once | Push the crate onto one, stand on the other | Stand on one each |
| `sequence` | Pads flash in an order. Hit them back in that order | Same | Same |
| `lights` | A hit flips a lamp and the lamps beside it. Get them all on | Same | Same |
| `switch` | A switch up high. Push the crate over, climb, hit it | Same | Same |
| `carry` | Pick a thing up and take it across. A hit knocks it out of your hands | Same | One carries, one guards |

### What each level got

| Level | Platforms and pits | Puzzle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 The Flyer | Crate stack | Lights (3 lamps) |
| 2 Lights Out | Gap in the roof | Sequence (3 pads, 3 long) |
| 3 Paper Chase | Stage | Plates (2 plates, 1 crate) |
| 4 Find Cass | Open manhole, truck | Carry (the gas can) |
| 5 The Footage | Scaffolds | Switch (the stage lights) |
| 6 Blue Room Blues | Stage | Sequence (4 pads, 4 long) |
| 7 The Switch | Gap, loading docks | Plates (2 plates, 1 crate) |
| 8 Cookout | Truck | Lights (4 lamps) |
| 9 The Courts | Gap, docks | Carry (the aux cord, up onto a dock) |
| 10 Overseas Call | Pit, crate stack | Sequence (4 pads, 5 long) |
| 11 The Venue | Stage | Plates (3 plates, 2 crates) |
| 12 Main Event | Stage | Sequence (4 pads, 6 long) |

All of this is data. To change a level, edit its entry in `const WORLD = {...}` near the bottom of `campaign.js`. Positions are fractions of the
zone's width (0 is the left edge, 1 the right), keyed by the zone's number.

### Drafts to replace

- **Art.** Every new world object (platforms, crates, plates, pads, lamps, switch, the carried things, the mixtape) is drawn in code as a
  placeholder. None of it came from the art pipeline.
- **Words.** The hints, the speaker lines and the puzzle names are first drafts. They follow the rule for story mode: fast, little text,
  callouts of one or two words where they can be.

## 3. Bugs fixed on the way

- A fighter that came to rest less than half a pixel above the floor never landed: stuck in the jump pose, could not act. Fixed in `physics0()`.
- Loose props no longer spawn on top of puzzle pads and lamps.

## 4. Tests added

All run in headless Edge against the local server (`PORT=8084 node serve.js`). Run one browser test at a time: several at once starve each
other and give false timeouts.

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `node tests/world-shots.js <out> <port> <level> [p2]` | The bot plays a level. A screenshot at each zone and each puzzle, and a log of falls, the tape and errors. Pass a second battler's id for co-op. |
| `node tests/world-trace.js <port> <level> <afterFrames> [rows] [p2]` | Prints where every player and enemy is, every 20 frames, from a given frame. For finding a stall. |
| `node tests/storysel-shot.js [out] [port] [size] [unlock]` | Screenshots of the story select, top and bottom, at one screen size. |
| `tests/brawl-bot.js` | The bot now climbs, jumps pits, goes round walls too tall to jump, and solves each puzzle. |

### Bot results

All twelve levels finish solo with no errors. Levels 3 and 9 also finish in two-player co-op. Level 4: the bot falls in the manhole five
times on the way through, which costs health but never blocks the level.

### The site banner

`brbrawl-site/app/components/UpdateBanner.js` shows "NEW GAME VERSION drops tomorrow, Tue 11/10 at 12 PM ET" with a countdown and takes
itself down at that time. To move the drop, change `DROP` (it is in UTC: 17:00 is noon Eastern). It went live on 2026-11-09. The game on
the site is still the old one: `public/play` is only updated by `node sync-game.js`, then a build and a deploy.

### Phone check

`node tests/menu-layout-check.js` at 390x844 and 844x390: every menu passes, story select included. One fix: on a phone held sideways the
battler select was sized for two rows of cards and now has three (K-Shine), so the top row slid under the header. The cards are sized for
three rows there, and names wrap onto two lines instead of being cut to "ROSENB...". The title screen sometimes reports a button a few
pixels off the left edge: that is its slide-in still running when the test measures, not a layout fault.
