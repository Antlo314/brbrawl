Before the final round, each battler spits one bar: tap any attack on the beat to land it. Clean bars win the crowd and start you with super. Off unless you turn it on; pause skips it.

VIBRATION

The phone buzzes when you get hit: a tick for a jab, a thud for a heavy, a double thump on a knockdown, a rumble for a super. Android phones (iPhone browsers don't allow it).

EFFECTS

FULL adds glow, depth and color to every stage. LIGHT keeps slower phones smooth. CALM turns off the impact flashes and camera kicks.

SPECIAL FX

The light show on specials, supers and the big hits, in each battler's colors. LOW is the default: smaller and fewer sparks. HIGH adds more detail. OFF never loads it.
